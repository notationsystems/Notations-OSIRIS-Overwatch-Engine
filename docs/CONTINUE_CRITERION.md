# Sea Dog Terminal — the continue criterion (shipping order S-7)

**Pre-registered 2026-08-27, BEFORE any researcher session has run.**
This document exists because thirty-four phases of investment make
continuation the default no matter what the evidence says, and this
project's whole discipline is that a number decided after seeing the
data isn't a measurement. The thresholds below were chosen while the
miss log is empty and no one but the builder has ever queried the
instrument. Amendments are legitimate only BEFORE the first session
(S-6); after it, these stand and the outcome is judged against them.

## The evaluation window

Ninety days from the first researcher session (S-6, day 0). The three
demand instruments are the evidence: the miss log (what the corpus
couldn't answer), the refusals digest (what it declined, with remedies),
and session telemetry (what was actually asked and inspected) — plus the
one observation only the operator can record: unprompted returns.

## CONTINUE — fund the S-5 maintenance cadence — if, within 90 days:

At least ONE of:
1. **Unprompted return**: any researcher (the S-6 researcher included)
   opens the instrument again without being asked to, on ≥3 distinct
   days. Visits that were requested, demoed, or scheduled do not count.
2. **A finding leaves the building**: ≥1 number, refusal, or divergence
   from this instrument appears in someone's own work product (a memo, a
   deck, a decision), recorded by the operator with a one-line note of
   what it changed.
3. **Demand with an address**: the miss log accumulates ≥10 distinct
   non-builder queries whose gap analysis names a registered source —
   evidence the corpus is being asked for MORE, not merely visited.

## FREEZE — keep, do not maintain — if:

Zero unprompted returns AND fewer than 10 non-builder miss-log entries
in the 90 days. The instrument is tagged at its final version, the
archive mirror refreshed one last time, CI left running (the guards keep
their meaning on a frozen artifact), and the maintenance cadence
formally lapses: corpus health will show the aging honestly, which is
what a frozen instrument should do. A frozen system that was honestly
retired is not a failed system; a maintained system nobody opens is.

## RETIRE the maintenance question early — if:

The S-6 researcher does not complete the afternoon (walks away, cannot
form a question the instrument could even mis-answer) — then the 90-day
window does not start; a second attempt with a different researcher is
warranted before any freeze decision, because a single no-show measures
the recruitment, not the instrument.

## Who decides, and when

The operator, at day 90 (or at the second failed S-6 attempt), against
this document as written. The builder's role at that point is to compute
the three instrument readings and present them WITHOUT recommendation —
the same separation as every measurement in this system: the instrument
reports, the human decides.

## Why these thresholds

Ten distinct queries is roughly one engaged afternoon plus incidental
returns — below it, the corpus was toured, not used. Three distinct
return days separates curiosity from utility. One finding-in-the-wild
outranks both, because an export into someone's own work is the only
POSITIVE signal of use this system can observe; everything else measures
absence. These are judgment calls made before the data existed — which
is the only time judgment calls are cheap.
